Full Face of Catrice: A Makeup Artist's Honest Review

By Nikol Pavlova – Professional Makeup Artist, Prague

I'm starting a new tradition: a full face from one brand. First up is Catrice. I'd collected a lot of products from its winter advent calendar, so I tested them on my whole face, up close, so you can see the pores and how the products really look on skin. Some I'll definitely recommend, others I definitely won't.

Most of the products come from the Catrice Twinkling Treasures advent calendar, and I added the HD Liquid Coverage foundation and the Melted Sun bronzer, which I've known for a long time. I skip brows in this episode; I tested brow products in the previous video.

How I tested

I apply everything the way most people would at home, and for some products I compare two methods, one on each side of the face. At the end I rate every product honestly.

Ratings reflect my skin and my style; with foundation, how your skin reacts matters a lot.

Products tested

Product photos: Catrice.

Lip prep: Juicy Lip Balm Champagne Fizz

Good value

Skin prep is minimal today; I only start by hydrating my lips. This balm caught my eye when I unboxed the calendar. It's Vaseline-like, gives a soft peachy tint, shines beautifully and isn't sticky. My lips still feel a little dry, but if you love glosses that shine without sticking, it's a great budget option.

Foundation: HD Liquid Coverage

Depends on skin · 6/10

Probably the first budget foundation I bought as a makeup artist, because everyone recommended it. I didn't get it at first; last year I realised it really is a good foundation. It's high coverage, can be applied thin or thick, and I like it for filming videos.

It has two downsides, though. It gives me breakouts every time: it contains niacinamide, and in this formula it irritates my skin and can be drying, so the foundation can sink into pores and look cakey. And it oxidises noticeably, like most matte foundations. Hydrating foundations do this less often.

On the plus side, its high pigment and grip mean it can double as concealer. I apply it under the eyes with the flat, double-angled Eyeshadow Brush Midnight Glamour from the calendar: a little stiff, but dense and decent for a cheap brush. I don't fully cover the bruise under my eye; I don't like heavily covered under-eyes. Many people put it on a par with Estée Lauder Double Wear; it layers, but over time it starts to look cakey on me.

Bronzer: Melted Sun 020 Beach Babe

Recommended

A well-known, much-loved cream bronzer. This isn't my first one and I use it for myself and at work. It's neutral, not too orange, and its trump card is the shade at this price. It's very blendable and doesn't grab straight away, so even beginners can work with it.

There's a rule of thumb: wax-based cream products are tricky over a matte foundation, because the wax can break down the matte base and leave patches. This bronzer has wax as its first ingredient and is still mega user-friendly. You can apply it heavily and take off the excess with a sponge, or keep a stronger contour.

Cream blush: Blush Stick Candy Cane

Questionable

A lovely tea-rose shade, and there's a lot of product. On one side of my face I applied it straight from the stick, the way most people do at home: it didn't blend easily, probably because it hadn't warmed up, and went patchy. On the other side I warmed it on my hand and applied it with a brush, which works better and needs no pressure.

But it clashed with the bronzer and created a muddy look, even though they're from the same brand. I fixed it with foundation on a brush. That showed how much HD Liquid Coverage oxidises: freshly applied it was clearly lighter.

Eyes: cream shadow, topper, pencils and mascara

Liquid Eyeshadow Golden Treasure

Not for me

A warm gold cream shadow; the calendar only had cream shadows. It set instantly, felt slightly cold on application (maybe alcohol evaporating) and feels sticky on the lid. It will probably last a long time, but cream eyeshadows aren't my thing.

Jelly Eyeshadow Topper Twinkling Crystal

Top pick

A fantastic glitter topper. Over the warm gold shadow it gives a cool sparkle and the effect is really pretty. I apply toppers with a finger; they layer more interestingly than with a brush.

Eyepencil Ruby Noel and Golden Eve

Skip · 1/10

The burgundy pencil has a beautiful shade, but it's dry, draws badly over cream shadow and a piece broke off. The liner came out very "approximate", so I smudged it into a smoky line. The light gold pencil for the inner corner broke too and doesn't stay on the waterline. One out of ten for the colour, and that's it.

The powder puff trick

When you draw liner, rest your hand on a powder puff instead of your face. You won't wipe off your base and your hand stays steady. Draw with your eyes open: first the line, then flick the tail out from it.

Glam & Doll Volume Mini Mascara

7/10

Applies easily and nicely. It promises volume; on me it lengthened more. My lashes are normal, so almost any mascara looks fine on me; wear time needs testing over a day. I give it a seven.

Baked blush and highlighters

Blush Baked Apple Pink

Top pick

A dome compact with a mirror that looks expensive. And the result: a beautiful pink I'd been looking for, with a subtle shimmer. With a very fluffy brush it gives a soft wash; with a denser, even synthetic brush it will be more pigmented. I also added a little to my eyes for depth. It's baked, so you can apply it very thinly, and when I travelled with it, it didn't break. In my experience similar products from Rare Beauty in the same type of packaging break terribly easily.

Highlighter Holly Jolly Shine

Recommended

A gold highlighter. The blush already shimmers, so you don't need much. I know and love Catrice highlighters: budget products with a beautiful glow, and under harder light the highlight will be even prettier.

Highlighter Glow Stick Glow Ho Ho

Good

A waxy highlighter stick, slightly cooler in tone, which I like more. I don't apply it straight to the face, because it would definitely start lifting the powder products. I take it on my fingers, it warms up and works better, and then you can even put it over powders without patches. It's 5.5 g, so it lasts a long time.

Lips: liners, lipsticks and tint

Lipliner Snowy Rose and Lipgloss Stick Merry Me?

Liner recommended

The warm beige liner is exactly the shade on the pack, soft, retractable, long-wearing and very comfortable to draw with. The glossy lipstick has no flavour, just wax and glycerin, and looks pretty.

Lipliner Winterberry and Juicy Lip Balm Burgundy Bliss

Not my shade

A red-pink combo. The liner feels long-wearing, maybe a little drying, but that's just my impression and needs longer wear. The balm is flavoured and scented, which I like. I just don't wear such bold, aggressive colours, and I rarely like them on anyone.

Peptide Lip Tint Merry Night Shine

Recommended

I generally like lip tints and this one is lovely: not sticky, not bitter, with a beautiful shine that suits soft makeup. My favourite combo was the beige Snowy Rose liner over the whole lip with this tint on top.

Overall impression

I expected more. At first glance the products promised a really interesting look, but the result was average to me, with no wow effect. This is one of the few videos on my channel where the products didn't excite me much, which is also why it's shorter than usual.

Still, I generally recommend Catrice. It's good makeup for anyone building their first makeup bag or wanting to try something new. With some products you just have to learn how to work. For comparison, see my full face of NYX review.

Pro tips from this test

  1. 1

    Warm cream products in your fingers.

    Waxy blushes and highlighters straight from the stick easily go patchy over matte foundation. Warmed on your fingers or applied with a brush, they melt in cleanly.

  2. 2

    Matte foundations oxidise.

    Choose the shade knowing it will deepen after a few minutes. For bridal makeup we check this at the trial.

  3. 3

    Baked products are perfect for travel.

    A baked blush and highlighter apply very thinly and won't shatter in your bag.

Frequently asked questions

Is Catrice HD Liquid Coverage foundation good?

+

It is high coverage, can be applied thin or thick and can double as concealer. But it oxidises noticeably, and for me the niacinamide in the formula causes breakouts. Overall I give it 6 out of 10.

Is Catrice HD Liquid Coverage a dupe for Estée Lauder Double Wear?

+

Many people put it on the same level. It layers, but on me it starts to look cakey over time, so it is debatable.

Which Catrice products from the test are worth buying?

+

The Baked Apple Pink baked blush, the Jelly Eyeshadow Topper, the Melted Sun 020 cream bronzer, the Holly Jolly Shine highlighter, the Peptide Lip Tint and the Snowy Rose lip liner.

How do I apply cream blush over matte foundation without patches?

+

Warm the product on the back of your hand or in your fingers and apply it with a brush without pressing. Straight from the stick onto a matte base it easily goes patchy.

Is Catrice good for beginners?

+

Yes. The Melted Sun bronzer is very blendable and forgiving, and the brand is great for building a first makeup bag. With some products you need to learn how to work, which we also cover in my makeup course.

How do I draw eyeliner without smudging my base?

+

Rest your hand on a powder puff instead of your face. Draw with your eyes open: first the line, then flick the tail out from it.
