+

For shoots, 2–3 weeks is usually enough, though last-minute slots can sometimes be arranged – just get in touch.

Do you travel for shoots outside Prague?

+

Yes. I am based in Prague and travel for shoots to other cities in the Czech Republic and across Europe.
