FAQ – popular questions

Where are you based and do you travel for bookings?

+

I am a professional makeup artist based in Prague, Czech Republic. I work across all districts of Prague – including Praha 1 (Old Town), Praha 2 (Vinohrady), and Praha 7 – and I also travel to other cities in the Czech Republic and across Europe for destination weddings, photo shoots and events.

What makeup services do you offer?

+

I offer wedding and bridal makeup, evening and cocktail styling, natural nude looks, prom and ball makeup, commercial and editorial makeup for photo shoots, and personalized one-on-one makeup courses for clients who want to learn professional techniques.

Do you speak English?

+

Yes. I provide makeup services fluently in English and Czech, which makes me a comfortable choice for international clients, expats living in Prague, and couples planning a destination wedding in the Czech Republic.

What is included in the wedding makeup package?

+

The wedding package includes a bridal trial before the wedding day, full wedding-day makeup application, false lashes, and a personal touch-up kit so your look stays fresh from the ceremony through the reception.

How much does makeup in Prague cost?

+

Prices depend on the type of service, location, and timing – bridal packages, evening makeup and commercial shoots are all priced individually. Please contact me with your event details and I will send you a personalized quote within 24 hours.

How far in advance should I book?

+

For weddings I recommend booking 6–12 months in advance, especially for the peak season from May to September. For evening makeup and shoots, 2–3 weeks is usually enough, though last-minute slots can sometimes be arranged – just get in touch.

What does a makeup course look like?

+

Courses are personalized one-on-one sessions tailored to your skill level and goals – from learning everyday makeup for yourself to professional techniques for aspiring MUAs. We cover skin prep, color theory, application techniques, and product selection using your own face as the canvas.

How can I book an appointment?

+

You can book online via Calendly, call or text +420 608 178 390, email pavlovanikol@gmail.com, or send a direct message on Instagram @nikol__mua. I usually reply within a few hours.
