How much does commercial makeup cost?

Commercial projects are priced individually by type of service, location and date. Send me the details and I will reply with a personalised quote within 24 hours.

How far in advance should I book a shoot?

For shoots, 2–3 weeks is usually enough, though last-minute slots can sometimes be arranged – just get in touch.

Do you travel for shoots outside Prague?

Yes. I am based in Prague and travel for shoots to other cities in the Czech Republic and across Europe.

Do you work in English?

Yes, I speak fluent English and Czech, so international teams are very welcome.
