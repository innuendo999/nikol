Beauty Empties: What I'd Repurchase and What I Wouldn't

By Nikol Pavlova – Professional Makeup Artist, Prague

I'm starting a new series: favourite products I've actually used up. I'd been collecting the bottles for more than a month, because I'd wanted to film this for a long time. If you see the same product two or three times, it means only one thing: I really love it. And for each one I tell you honestly whether I'd buy it again.

My top favourites

  1. Académie Hydraderm: the toner I have used for ten years
  2. Image Skincare Vital C: the best vitamin C serum I know
  3. Paula's Choice SPF 50 with vitamin C: the best SPF I've ever had
  4. Caudalie Beauty Elixir: a face mist I have repurchased for over five years
  5. Bumble and Bumble: the hair sun protection I needed long ago
  6. Rituals body butter and the Benefit brow pen

How I choose

I used up everything in this article to the last drop. Some products have been in my routine for years, others were a one-time buy. As a makeup artist I test a lot of products, but only a few make it to the very end. I use many of them on clients too, so I also judge how they perform at work.

Skincare

Académie Hydraderm Tonique Hydratant

Will repurchase

I've been praising this toner for ten years and I've got a lot of people hooked on it. After it, your skin feels like it has had a drink of water. It's light and watery, and depending on the line it cleanses, hydrates or both. I recommend the classic Hydraderm for every skin type. The pink line for sensitive skin did nothing for me, though. If your skin is dry and needs nourishing as well as hydrating, it may not be enough; try a milky toner instead.

Medicube: two-phase toner

Will repurchase, but not for summer

A two-phase toner for dehydrated skin that needs nourishing. I don't use it in summer; I find it leaves a film and feels heavy in the heat. For the colder months, though, I'll definitely buy it again: it works beautifully with everything I've layered it with. In summer I switch to the lighter toner from my summer routine.

Image Skincare Vital C Hydrating Anti-Aging Serum

Will repurchase

A cosmetologist in Kyiv recommended it to me when I was about twenty. I've bought it every year since, with the odd break, because it isn't cheap. I've tried other vitamin C serums and nothing is better. I love its scent, it hydrates beautifully and doesn't irritate my skin at all. If you want a stronger vitamin C, you may find it too gentle. My combo from many videos: the Académie toner, this serum, then a cream.

Bobbi Brown Vitamin Enriched Face Base

Will repurchase

I love its scent and texture. It's slightly rich and hydrating and suits almost every skin type except very oily skin. It works as a makeup base or simply as a cream. The small size is my workhorse: one lives at the studio, one at home, and I've gone through a lot of them because I really use it on clients.

Caudalie Beauty Elixir

Will repurchase

I've used it for at least five years. I spray this face mist before, during and after makeup. I use it on myself more than anything else, and a little less on clients because its fresh, herbal scent isn't for everyone. The minis live in my handbag and my beach bag. Once your face is powdered and you don't need extreme wear, it can replace a setting spray: it doesn't tighten the skin and it's wonderful in the heat.

d'Alba White Truffle First Spray Serum

Will repurchase

A great spray that smells completely different from Caudalie. It contains a little oil, more like a serum, so I don't use it on oily skin. For every other skin type it's excellent: it refreshes makeup and melts the layers together, especially when the makeup is set with a lot of powder and looks dry.

Sunscreen

Paula's Choice 5% Vitamin C Sheer Moisturizer SPF 50

Will repurchase

This is the third video where I mention it, and it's still the best SPF I've ever had. It's very light and pleasant, with a small percentage of vitamin C that shouldn't irritate but still works. I pair it either with the Bobbi Brown base when I want lots of hydration, or simply with a toner. I show how I wear it under light makeup in my summer makeup article.

Round Lab Birch Juice Moisturizing Sun Cream SPF 50+

A good choice

I've used it for over a year and still have a few in stock. The texture is very light, though a bit heavier than Paula's Choice. It suits a lot of people and many of my friends use it regularly.

Mixsoon centella SPF 50

Won't repurchase

My anti-favourite. It's heavy, I'd even say greasy and sticky. At least two thirds of the jar is left. I tried it on clients too, but every time all I feel is the greasiness.

Lips and barrier repair

Laneige Lip Sleeping Mask

Will repurchase

An endless favourite that has been in my kit since I moved to the Czech Republic. The purple one is my favourite: it reminds me of grape gummy bears. The chocolate one has a nice shade and taste, but I don't use it on clients: brown doesn't suit everyone, and on very fair skin it looks odd, and so does the whole makeup.

La Roche-Posay Cicaplast Baume B5+

Will repurchase

A cream that repairs the skin barrier. I sometimes use it as an overnight mask, about once every couple of weeks, not regularly, because it can overwhelm the skin. It's great when your barrier is struggling or you've overdone active ingredients, and in winter for dry skin. It isn't expensive and is worth every penny. The catch: it's only good for six months after opening, and I've never managed to finish one. With skincare I respect the period after opening even more strictly than with makeup.

Cleansing and makeup removal

Allies of Skin cleanser

Depends on your skin

Pricey, but not crazy. It suits combination and oily skin best; for dry, especially dehydrated skin it would be too much. The texture is great and it's almost unscented, which I love. I hate cleansers with a strong scent that you can still smell in bed.

Needly cleansing balm

Won't repurchase

Its scent was so herbal that after washing it reminded me of mould. I'd go to bed wondering where the smell was coming from. It was coming from me.

Bioderma Sensibio H2O

Will repurchase

My long-time favourite, which I alternate with Payot micellar water because they're very similar. I buy whichever comes in a big bottle on sale. I don't wash my face with micellar water, but at work it's a consumable. One of the best micellar waters I've tried for the price.

Hair

Shu Uemura shampoo

Depends on your hair

I tried a sample by chance and thought it was the best thing that had happened to my hair. I used up the shampoo and the mask. It cleanses and smooths beautifully, but on my porous, coloured, curly hair it was only okay and my curls twisted strangely. For straight hair I highly recommend it. I liked the scent and the results; I liked the price less, so it isn't a full favourite.

Lakmé hair mask

Will repurchase

My favourite for value, already on my second jar with a new one waiting. A hairdresser and colourist recommended it to me. Masks are either hydrating and runnier, or nourishing and thick like a paste. I use this one almost every time I wash my hair (every few days). It really restores the hair and smells lovely.

Redken One United

Won't repurchase

A leave-in that promises 25 benefits in one bottle: heat protection, smoothing and a protective barrier. I used it up, but nothing extraordinary happened, and it isn't cheap. I don't believe in products "for all hair types". My damaged, dry hair needs something different.

Bumble and Bumble Hairdresser's Invisible Oil Primer

Will repurchase

Already my second bottle. It's a leave-in, primer and hair sun protection in one. Every year I spend about a month in Asia, and after my first trip my neutral brown hair turned reddish: it burnt under my scooter helmet, and I didn't rinse off the salt water after the sea. Now I know you need to rinse your hair with fresh water after every swim and apply a moisturising leave-in. I use this spray in Prague too whenever I'm out in the sun.

Holiday tip

After the sea, always rinse your hair with fresh water and spray on a leave-in with UV protection. Salt and sun burn even hair that isn't coloured.

Body

Rituals body butter

Will repurchase

I call its scent "the scent of an expensive woman": you smell of cleanliness and luxury. It isn't sweet, overly fresh or heavy, just soft. Subtle scents suit me better than smelling of everything at once. And it lasts amazingly well. There are small jars if you want to try it first.

La Sultane de Saba Ayurveda body lotion

Won't repurchase

A beautiful scent, but it didn't moisturise my body at all. And unlike Rituals, the scent fades very quickly.

Makeup

Estée Lauder Futurist

For dry and mature skin

A foundation for dry, very dry and mature skin. I used to wear it in summer; now it feels heavy on me, but you can mix it with other products, including tints. A hydrating foundation that has no rival when applied with your hands. My most used shades are 2N1 (neutral, a little deeper for summer) and 1W2 (properly warm, also for tanned skin). Off-season I use 1N2, lighter and neutral. I mostly choose neutral shades because many of my clients have olive or mixed olive undertones.

Benefit Brow Microfilling Pen

Will repurchase

My makeup favourite, and I'll definitely buy it again. My brows today are done with it and they look really good. The bristles are unusual, and at first I didn't get them, but they're very easy to draw with.

Physicians Formula Eye Booster Liner

A favourite

I bought it for freckles, but ended up using it on brows. The warm Universal Brown shade doesn't change during the day. With NYX Taupe brow pencils I've seen brows turn green, as I mention in my NYX review. With brides that's a nightmare: they send you the photos and the brows are green. That's why for bridal makeup I choose products that don't oxidise.

Dior Forever Skin Correct

Love it, not buying now

A hydrating concealer I started my career with. I have it in three shades for work: 1CR, 2N and 3WP; I also really like 1N. Shade 2N oxidised and darkened over time. I know it's expensive, but I really love it. I'm only not repurchasing because I have plenty of other concealers.

Bonus: Philips Sonicare toothbrush

Will repurchase

I've used Philips Sonicare electric toothbrushes for seven years and my life splits into before and after. The battery has never let me down, and if one breaks, the service centre replaces it under warranty. I use it at least twice a day, more often with aligners, so I understand it wears out.

Will repurchase

Three things to take away

  1. 1

    Fewer products, but proven ones.

    I only finish what genuinely works. Hype isn't enough; what matters is how a product behaves on your skin week after week.

  2. 2

    Skin and hair type decide.

    Allies of Skin is great for oily skin, Hydraderm isn't enough for dry skin and Shu Uemura loves straight hair. Truly universal products barely exist.

  3. 3

    Watch the period after opening.

    Cicaplast lasts six months. With skincare, the dates after opening matter even more than with makeup.

Frequently asked questions

Which skincare products does a makeup artist repurchase?

+

The Académie Hydraderm toner, Image Skincare Vital C serum, Bobbi Brown Vitamin Enriched Face Base, Paula's Choice SPF 50 and Caudalie Beauty Elixir face mist. I've used all of them for years and finished them to the last drop.

What is the best SPF to wear under makeup?

+

For me it's Paula's Choice 5% Vitamin C Sheer Moisturizer SPF 50: light and no film under makeup. Round Lab Birch Juice SPF 50+ is a good alternative. Mixsoon SPF 50 was too greasy for me.

Can a face mist replace a setting spray?

+

Yes, if your makeup doesn't need extreme wear. Caudalie Beauty Elixir refreshes the skin without tightening it. For dry skin and heavily powdered makeup, d'Alba First Spray Serum works well; not for oily skin.

Is Shu Uemura shampoo worth it?

+

For straight hair, yes: it cleanses and smooths beautifully. On my porous, coloured, curly hair it was only okay, and for the price it isn't my favourite.

How do I protect my hair from sun and sea?

+

After every swim, rinse your hair with fresh water and apply a leave-in with UV protection, such as Bumble and Bumble Hairdresser's Invisible Oil Primer.

Which foundation is best for dry or mature skin?

+

Estée Lauder Futurist: a hydrating foundation that also applies beautifully with your hands. For olive undertones I choose neutral shades such as 2N1 or 1N2.
