(FAQ)

Questions about ball makeup

Didn’t find your answer? Message me, I usually reply within a few hours.

How much does ball makeup cost in Prague?

Ball makeup costs CZK 2,500, a ball hairstyle CZK 2,500, and makeup and hair together only CZK 4,500 – you save CZK 500. Travel is charged separately depending on the location.

Do you also do ball hairstyles?

Yes. I do makeup and hair in one appointment – from an elegant updo to Hollywood waves. Send me a photo of your dress and some inspiration.

How far ahead should I book?

In ball season (January–February) I recommend 4–6 weeks ahead; Saturdays disappear fastest. Outside the season, 2–3 weeks is usually enough.

Will the makeup last all night?

Yes. I prep the skin, set the makeup in layers and use waterproof products. I leave you the lipstick for small touch-ups.

How long does it take?

Makeup alone about 60–75 minutes, makeup and hair 2 hours.

Will you come to my home or hotel?

Yes, I travel all over Prague, and outside Prague by arrangement. Travel is charged separately depending on the location.

Can you also do my mum or friends?

Yes, I’m happy to get several people ready at once – just give the number when booking and I’ll plan the timing so you are all ready on time.

Can I send inspiration and a photo of my dress?

Definitely – it helps fine-tune the colours and intensity of the look.

I have sensitive skin – is that a problem?

Not at all. Let me know in advance and I’ll adapt the products.

How can I book an appointment?

Book online via Calendly, call or text +420 608 178 390, email pavlovanikol@gmail.com, or send a message on Instagram @nikol__mua. I usually reply within a few hours.
