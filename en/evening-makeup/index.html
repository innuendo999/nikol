FAQ – evening makeup questions

How far in advance should I book?

+

For evening makeup and shoots, 2–3 weeks is usually enough, though last-minute slots can sometimes be arranged – just get in touch.

How much does evening makeup cost in Prague?

+

Prices depend on the type of service, location and timing. Send me your event details and I will reply with a personalised quote within 24 hours.

Do you come to me?

+

Yes. I work across all districts of Prague – including Praha 1 (Old Town), Praha 2 (Vinohrady) and Praha 7 – and travel to other cities in the Czech Republic and across Europe.

Do you speak English?

+

Yes, I speak fluent English and Czech, so international clients and expats in Prague are very welcome.

How can I book an appointment?

+

Book online via Calendly, call or text +420 608 178 390, email pavlovanikol@gmail.com, or send a message on Instagram @nikol__mua. I usually reply within a few hours.
