Event makeup for you and your company

from a gala to a conference

At an event you want to look great without the worry. For you I do makeup and hair for a gala, a party, a birthday, an anniversary, a premiere or a hen party. For companies and organisers I provide makeup for speakers and hosts before a conference or filming, group makeup for your team and touch-ups during the event. How much does event makeup cost?

Per person: makeup CZK 2,500, hair CZK 2,500, makeup and hair together only CZK 4,500 – you save CZK 500. Travel is charged separately depending on the location.

How many people can you do?

It depends on the time we have. Allow roughly 60–75 minutes for makeup alone and 2 hours for makeup with hair per person – I plan the schedule around that.

Can you stay at the event for touch-ups?

Yes, by arrangement. Scope and price are agreed when you book.

Will you come to the venue?

Yes – to hotels, offices and venues anywhere in Prague, and outside Prague by arrangement. Travel is charged separately depending on the location.

Do you speak English?

Yes – English, Czech and Ukrainian, so international teams and foreign guests are very welcome.

How can I book an appointment?

Book online via Calendly, call or text +420 608 178 390, email pavlovanikol@gmail.com, or send a message on Instagram @nikol__mua. I usually reply within a few hours.
