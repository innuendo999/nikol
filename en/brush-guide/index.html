(About the guide)

Which brush for what, and how to use it

the brush list isn’t mandatory – what matters is knowing which shape you need

I’m Ukrainian and I live in Prague; beauty was always my hobby and today it’s my work. In this guide I put the brushes I really use: for foundation and concealer, powder and blush, eyes, brows and lips. For each one you’ll find what it’s for, which movement to use and my tip. You don’t need the same brands – the guide teaches you to recognise the brush shape and bristle you need.
